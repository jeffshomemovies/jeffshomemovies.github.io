Lauren Kolar

@lolas.critter.farm

Lauren Kolar is an interdisciplinary artist and fabricator based in Brooklyn, NY. Informed by a childhood full of crafting, her work celebrates the joy of making work with her hands, as well as unconventional applications of accessible materials. Combining analog and digital techniques gives her work a unique quality that takes 3-dimensional objects and gives them new life in the digital space, while maintaining a crucial tactility. Her work often draws from creature features, cartoons, video games, and the whimsy of the natural world.

Alex Seewald

@a_seewald

My name is Alex and I am a NYC based queer and neurodivergent artist. I have a passion for channeling my creative skills to improve the world around me. I just finished serving a term with AmeriCorps teaching art to high school students in Harlem. My work primarily speaks to issues of consumerism, the environment, and nostalgia. Most of my materials are conventionally seen as ‘garbage’–including found objects, product packaging, and other things usually thrown away. My portfolio is available at alexseewald.weebly.com

Bennett Bailey

@bforbennett

Bennett Bailey (b.1996) is a Black trans painter, born and working in Brooklyn, NY. His work visualizes abstract bodies that defy gender binaries, infuses with his interests in the supernatural, spirituality, mythology and trans experience. He finds traces of fantasy in reality and expands on them, creating beacons of reflection and joy in the midst of chaos.

Samuel J Pitzer

@dummy.clt

I am an artist and writer from Charlotte, NC, currently residing in Philadelphia, PA. My mediums are a mix of acrylic, colored pencil, ink, watercolor and oil pastel. My work deals mostly with darker topics and features characters in states of agony, depression, and overall unease. As my practice has developed I've come to turn towards a more political message. It's hard to be someone that creates things and ignore the turmoil. Perhaps it's the artists duty to be the first to call out evil and corruption for what it is outright, no matter the consequences.
